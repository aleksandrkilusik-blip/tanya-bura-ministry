My story

My name is Tanya Bura.

I serve with Ukraine for Christ, the Ukrainian branch of Campus Crusade for Christ International.

In a nation transformed by full-scale war, people need God’s hope more than ever. My calling is to help young people encounter the Gospel, grow as disciples, and become a witness for Christ wherever God has placed them.

“The harvest is plentiful but the workers are few.”
Matthew 9:37–38
Our purpose

Win. Build. Send.

We believe spiritual leaders can influence the future of Ukraine. Our mission is to walk with people from their first encounter with the Gospel to a life that multiplies faith in their own community.

WIN

Reach people with the Gospel through genuine relationships, student groups, and meaningful conversations.

BUILD

Help new believers grow toward spiritual maturity as multiplying disciples of Jesus.

SEND

Equip them to be a witness of Christ in the places where they study, work, and serve.

What I do

Student ministry that meets people where they are.

My ministry includes student discipleship and gatherings, media and communications for Ukraine for Christ, partnerships with seminaries, and Bible Plans for Cru.

Student community

Small groups, events, and one-to-one discipleship create room for students to ask honest questions and grow in faith.

Faith together

Meaningful conversations and Gospel-centered friendships.

Media & Bible plans

Digital content that meets students where their attention already is.

A changing mission

The war changed us — and our mission changed too.

Alongside student ministry, Ukraine for Christ offers spiritual, intellectual, and social support for children and teenagers. Mobile libraries help make safe spaces for learning, imagination, and hope available where they are needed.
